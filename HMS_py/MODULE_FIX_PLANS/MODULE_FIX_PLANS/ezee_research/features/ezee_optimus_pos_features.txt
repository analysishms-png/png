- Item-wise Discount + Bill Discount + Percentage

- Customer DB + engagement (SMS/email), CRM lite
- Reports: sales, item-wise, shift, day-close settlement, tax
- Pricing: Basic ~$45/outlet/month, Advanced ~$60/outlet/month, 14-day free trial
