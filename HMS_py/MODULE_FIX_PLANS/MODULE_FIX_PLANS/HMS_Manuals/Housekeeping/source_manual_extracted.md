[Heading 1] What is Analysis HMS?
Analysis HMS (Hotel Management System) is a comprehensive cloud-based software solution designed specifically for the Indian hospitality industry. It streamlines all hotel operations including Front Office, Housekeeping, Laundry, POS, Inventory, and Reporting from a single unified platform accessible from any device via web browser.
[Heading 1] Housekeeping Module Overview
The Housekeeping Module in Analysis HMS is a powerful tool that enables complete management of hotel room cleaning, staff assignment, room status tracking, and laundry services. It is organized into four main sections:

[Heading 1] QR Login Workflow  Housekeeping
QR-based login is the core innovation of Analysis HMS. No app installation required — it runs in the phone's browser.
[TABLE]
Role | Housekeeper (Floor Staff)
Device | Any smartphone with camera
Login Method | QR Code → Browser auto-opens → Login credentials
[/TABLE]
[Heading 2] Complete QR Workflow — Step by Step
Step 1: Approach Assigned Room: Walk to the room shown in your assignment list.
Step 2: Scan QR Code: Use phone camera or QR scanner app to scan the code on the door.
Step 3: Browser Opens Automatically: The login page loads in your mobile browser — no app needed.
Step 4: Enter Credentials: Username: Your registered Housekeeping User ID. Password: Assigned by Admin.
Step 5: View Assignment Details: Room No, Floor, Room Type, Priority, Cleaning Type, Supervisor Name are displayed.
Step 6: Capture Before Photo: Take a photo of the room BEFORE cleaning starts. Upload via Camera or Gallery.
Step 7: Click START CLEANING: Timer T1 starts. Room status changes to Cleaning In Progress.
Step 8: Execute Cleaning SOPs: Follow the physical cleaning checklist (Bedroom → Bathroom → Amenities).
Step 9: Damage Report: If any damage is found, click Damage Report, describe item, take photo.
Step 10: Lost & Found: If guest item found, click Lost & Found, enter description, time found.
Step 11: Digital Checklist: Tick off all checklist items on the mobile screen.
Step 12: Submit — FINISH CLEANING: Timer T2 stops. TAT (Turnaround Time) calculated. Room status → Inspection Pending.
[Heading 2] What Happens After Submit?
Room status immediately updates to 'Inspection Pending' on the Supervisor's dashboard
Supervisor receives visual notification that the room is ready for QA
TAT (T2 - T1) is recorded for the productivity report
Housekeepers are freed and their next assignment appears automatically
